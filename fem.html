Finite Element IVP/BVP Solver

Custom finite element software for general systems of nonlinear initial and boundary value problems.

Overview

I am currently developing a custom Finite Element-based program for the solution of general systems of nonlinear IVPs and BVPs. This includes hpk-framework capabilities with a posteriori error computations.

Future updates will cover formulation, solver design, verification studies, and progress on adaptive error computation workflows.
